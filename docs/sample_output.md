# Modern Room Layouts with Medium Budget in INR

Note: Pricing is indicative and may vary depending on specific choices & location, as it's based on generic estimations.

## Room Layout 1: Minimalist Chic - for the introspective soul

- Space: A compact bedroom or study area (approx. 10-12 sq meters).
- Colors: White, gray, black with splashes of blue or green accents to inject energy and personality.
- Furniture:
    - Sofa/Lounging Chair: A single, sleek modern sofa/chair in high-quality fabric like linen or velvet (INR 5000-12,000).
    - Table: Minimal table with a clean wood finish and metal accents for reading (INR 3000-8000).
        - Style Recommendation: Round or square table with one shelf for books/magazines.
- Accessories:
    - Reading Nook: Create a cozy corner with a comfortable armchair, throw blanket, and floor lamp (INR 2500-10,000).
    - Art & Décor: Abstract prints or geometric shapes on the wall will enhance the modern feel (INR 1000-3000).
    - Plant: A small potted plant for a touch of green life (INR 500-1500).

### Layout Summary:
- Clean lines
- Functional furniture
- Minimalist decor
- Peaceful and focused space for reading

## Room Layout 2: Bright & Open - for the creative soul

- Space: Living room with an open kitchen area (approx. 15-18 sq meters).
- Colors: Warm neutrals (beige, cream, light grey) combined with pops of vibrant color in cushions/throws (e.g., coral, teal) to enhance energy and comfort.
- Furniture:
    - Sofa: An oversized sofa with clean lines and modular sections for flexibility (INR 15000-30000).
    - Table: A glass coffee table or a wooden table with metal legs, providing space for books/snacks and drinks (INR 4000-10,000).
        - Style Recommendation: A rectangular table with ample surface area.
- Accessories:
    - Reading Nook: A plush armchair with a soft throw and a side table for reading essentials (INR 6000-20,000).
    - Art & Décor: Introduce geometric art prints or a large abstract painting (INR 2000-5000).
    - Mirror: A large mirror to create an illusion of more space and reflect light (INR 1000-3000).

### Layout Summary:
- Ideal for hosting friends and family
- Dedicated, comfortable reading nook

## Room Layout 3: Industrial Chic - for the adventurous soul

- Space: A small bedroom or den (approx. 8-10 sq meters).
- Colors: Black, white, grey with accents of red/orange/burgundy to add warmth and visual interest.
- Furniture:
    - Sofa: A black leather sofa or a fabric sofa with a distressed finish for an industrial feel (INR 12000-30000).
    - Table: A metal table with concrete base, exposed pipes, etc., as the focal point and functional area (INR 5000-12000).
        - Style Recommendation: A coffee table in a modern industrial style - concrete, metal, wood elements.
- Accessories:
    - Reading Nook: A comfortable armchair with a throw blanket, placed against the wall, ideal for reading (INR 3000-15000).
    - Art & Décor: Industrial-style posters or framed black and white photos (INR 2000-5000).

### Layout Summary:
- Industrial aesthetic
- Focus on functionality and comfort
- Ideal for creative individuals who want a modern space
